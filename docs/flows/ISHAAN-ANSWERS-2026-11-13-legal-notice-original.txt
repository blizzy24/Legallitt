LEGALITT – LEGAL NOTICE FLOW

Final Answers and Development Instructions (Revised)

1. Client-Side Decisions

C1. Reply Update After Delivery

Replace “Get Reply Update” with a reply-management facility.

* The client should be able to view replies received from the noticee.
* Admin/legal team can upload a reply received through other channels.
* The client can also upload a reply received directly by them.
* The uploaded reply must be visible to the client, advocate and admin.
* Generate an in-app notification whenever a reply is uploaded.

Do not claim that Legalitt automatically receives replies from the noticee.

C2. Sender Details – Father’s Name, Mother’s Name and Husband’s Name

Make the following changes to the sender details section:

* Father’s Name
* Mother’s Name
* Husband’s Name

All three must be separate fields wherever applicable.

Remove the Age field completely. Age must not appear as a mandatory or optional field anywhere in the sender details section or the legal notice form.

C3. Noticee Type – Person / Company

Provide two options:

* Individual / Person
* Company / Organisation

For a company, provide an additional field for the designation or concerned person through whom the notice is addressed, wherever applicable.

C4. Issue Description

The client must provide a brief description of the dispute.

* Issue description: Mandatory.
* Maximum length: 2,000 characters.
* Amount involved: Optional.
* Key date: Optional.

For Cheque Bounce, capture cheque number, cheque date, amount, bank details and cheque return date as mandatory information.

C5. Identity Verification

Implement the previously approved identity-verification arrangements, including the approved Aadhaar OTP and Video KYC alternatives.

* Identity information must be masked wherever appropriate.
* Verification status must reflect the actual verification completed.
* Do not display “KYC Verified” merely because a document has been uploaded.
* DigiLocker must not be a mandatory dependency.

C6. Document Requirements According to Notice Type

Document upload categories should change dynamically according to the notice type selected.

* Property Dispute: Property papers, sale deed, agreements and screenshots.
* Money Recovery: Invoices, bills, agreements and payment proof.
* Cheque Bounce: Cheque copy and bank return memo as mandatory documents.
* Tenant Issue: Rent agreement, rent receipts and relevant communications.
* Family / Consumer / Other: Relevant supporting documents.

The client must also be able to upload additional documents.

C7. Client Does Not Respond to Draft

* First reminder after 24 hours.
* Second reminder after 72 hours.
* After seven days, place the case in the admin’s pending-client-response list.
* Admin may contact the client.
* The notice must never be treated as approved automatically.

C8. Change Requests and Revisions

Allow the client to request revisions to the draft.

After three revision rounds, generate an alert for the admin so that the case can be monitored.

Do not automatically reject further revision requests merely because three rounds have been completed.

C9. Failed Delivery and Additional Courier Charges

If delivery fails:

* A new courier dispatch to an alternative postal address will attract another ₹179 courier fee.
* Providing an alternative email address or WhatsApp number will not attract an additional ₹179 fee.
* Inform the client of the applicable charge before proceeding.

C10. Advocate-Client Chat Duration

The proposed one-hour live-chat restriction must not be implemented.

* Client and advocate can communicate through the case chat from acceptance until the advocate submits the completed notice to Legalitt.
* Once the advocate submits the notice to admin, the advocate’s work on that case is concluded.
* The advocate has no further dispatch responsibility.
* Admin/legal team takes over the remaining verification, signature and delivery process.

There must be no one-hour chat expiry or one-hour live-session restriction.

C11. Upload Noticee’s Reply After Delivery

Implement a reply section in the client’s case tracking page.

* Client can upload a reply received directly from the noticee.
* Admin/legal team can upload a reply received through other channels.
* Uploaded replies must be accessible to the client and assigned advocate.
* Generate an in-app notification when a reply is uploaded.
* Update the case activity/timeline with “Reply Received”.

Do not create a separate Legal Notice Response service at present.

2. Advocate-Side Decisions

A1. Draft Deadline

Display the actual draft submission deadline.

* Urgent: 5 days.
* Ordinary: 10 days.

The deadline should be calculated from payment confirmation and displayed in the advocate’s dashboard.

A2. Advocate Earnings

Display the advocate’s actual base fee/share.

The platform fee of 25% and GST are additional amounts charged to the client and must not be deducted from the advocate’s base fee.

The ₹179 courier fee is not part of advocate earnings.

A3. Ownership of ₹179 Courier Fee

The ₹179 courier fee belongs to Legalitt because Legalitt’s legal team is responsible for arranging and bearing the courier delivery cost.

It must not be credited to the advocate’s wallet.

A4. AI Document Summary Card

Display the AI Document Summary card in the advocate’s dashboard, even if its functionality is not yet operational.

* The card must be visible in the advocate’s case details.
* Show the title “AI Document Summary”.
* The card may display an appropriate placeholder message such as “AI document summary will be available here.”
* The card must not be removed or hidden merely because the functionality is not yet developed.
* Do not display fabricated summaries or AI-generated results.
* Actual functionality can be implemented in a subsequent development phase.

A5. AI-Generated Drafting

Continue with the existing AI drafting functionality.

* Advocate can choose AI Generate Draft.
* Advocate can also prepare the notice manually.
* AI should use facts and information supplied in the client’s application and uploaded documents.
* Advocate must be able to edit and review the generated draft before sending it to the client.

AI-generated content must not be dispatched without advocate review.

A6. Advocate Signature

The final notice must carry the advocate’s authorised signature.

Implement the approved signature workflow:

1. Advocate’s Aadhaar-based e-signature; or
2. Advocate’s signature uploaded and approved for use under the approved workflow.

Where an uploaded signature is used:

* Its placement must be fixed after the closing lines of the notice.
* It must not be freely movable.
* A simulated signature must never be represented as a completed Aadhaar e-signature.

The admin/legal team is responsible for coordinating the final signature and dispatch process.

A7. Advocate’s Responsibility After Submitting Draft

Once the advocate submits the client-approved notice to admin:

* Advocate’s responsibility for that case ends.
* Advocate’s case moves to the submitted/completed stage on their dashboard.
* Advocate does not verify the postal address for dispatch.
* Advocate does not arrange courier, email or WhatsApp delivery.
* Advocate does not upload proof of delivery.

All further responsibility rests with Legalitt’s legal/admin team.

A8. Legal Notice Response Service

Do not incorporate a separate Legal Notice Response service at present.

This refers to drafting a reply to a legal notice received by a client, which is different from sending a legal notice.

It may be considered as a separate service in a future development phase.

3. Admin-Side Decisions

AD1. WhatsApp Dispatch

The Legalitt legal/admin team will be responsible for WhatsApp dispatch.

For initial implementation:

* Admin sends the notice manually through the Legalitt business WhatsApp number.
* Admin records dispatch date and time.
* Admin updates delivery status.
* Client receives an in-app notification.

Automated WhatsApp Business API integration can be considered separately.

AD2. Email Dispatch

Provide email dispatch functionality through the Legalitt platform.

* Admin initiates dispatch.
* Final signed PDF is attached.
* System records recipient email address and dispatch date/time.
* Email bounce or failure must be recorded.
* Client receives an in-app status notification.

AD3. Failed Delivery

When any delivery method fails:

* Admin records the failure reason.
* Client receives an in-app notification.
* Client can provide an alternative address or contact detail.
* Admin verifies the new information before dispatching again.

The additional courier charge rule in C9 will apply.

AD4. E-sign Integration

The application must support the approved advocate signature workflow.

Until the actual Aadhaar e-sign provider is integrated, a test environment may be used for development.

* Clearly identify simulated transactions.
* Never show a test signature as a genuine Aadhaar e-sign.
* Maintain signature records and relevant timestamps.
* Do not represent the notice as authentically e-signed unless the actual signing process has been completed.

AD5. Proof of Delivery

For courier delivery:

* Admin must upload the Proof of Delivery (POD) when available.
* Record delivery date, time and recipient details.
* Make the POD available to the client for viewing and downloading.
* Update case status after delivery is confirmed.

For email and WhatsApp, maintain their respective dispatch and delivery records. Do not generate fictitious courier PODs for electronic delivery.

⸻

4. Business, Payment and Cancellation Policies

P1. Cancellation Before Advocate Acceptance

If the client cancels before an advocate accepts the request, the client receives a full refund.

P2. Cancellation After Advocate Acceptance but Before Dispatch

The client may request cancellation.

Admin will examine the work already performed and decide the applicable refund amount on a case-by-case basis.

The refund decision must be recorded in the case history.

P3. Cancellation After Dispatch

Once the notice has been dispatched, cancellation will not be permitted.

The client may contact Legalitt Support for assistance.

P4. Advocate Misses the Deadline

If the advocate fails to complete the draft within the prescribed deadline:

* Admin receives an overdue notification.
* Admin may contact the advocate.
* Admin may extend the deadline with recorded reasons and notify the client.
* Admin may reassign the case if necessary.
* Admin may grant a partial refund on a case-by-case basis.

There must not be an automatic full refund merely because the deadline has passed.

P5. Client Does Not Respond

Follow the reminder procedure:

1. First reminder after 24 hours.
2. Second reminder after 72 hours.
3. Admin intervention after seven days.

The notice must never be automatically approved due to client inactivity.

P6. Admin Finds Errors During Final Verification

The admin/legal team will examine the final notice before dispatch.

If any correction is necessary:

* Admin returns the draft to the advocate with specific reasons.
* Advocate makes the necessary corrections.
* Revised notice is submitted again to admin.
* Admin conducts final verification before proceeding.

P7. Multiple Delivery Methods

The client may select one or more delivery methods:

* Courier
* Email
* WhatsApp

Maintain separate dispatch and delivery records for each selected method.

If courier and email are both selected, the system must maintain separate courier and email statuses.

The case must not be shown as successfully delivered through every method merely because one method succeeds.
