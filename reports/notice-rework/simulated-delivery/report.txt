27 October 2026 — admin delivery simulator and document-access audit

Read-access finding: DocumentService.url calls locked(), which joins the case
and scopes access to its client, assigned advocate, or an admin. owner_id is
not a read permission. Only deletion additionally checks uploader/admin.
Regression: an admin-owned one-page POD is readable by the client and assigned
advocate; unrelated client/advocate receive 404 before an S3 URL is issued.

A third dev demo account, demo-admin@legalitt.invalid, was seeded through the
existing Cognito AdminCreateUser/AdminSetUserPassword path, with verified email
and a local users row. Seeding requires ENVIRONMENT=dev, non-production Node,
a dev-tagged Cognito pool, and the configured password secret. Public phone
OTP still accepts only client/advocate roles; it cannot sign in as admin.

The simulator authenticates using the normal admin login and calls the normal
presign, S3 POST, document-completion, and delivery endpoints. A scoped in-memory
provenance ticket never replaces Cognito authentication or role checks. It
binds the admin, case and uploaded document, expires after five minutes, and
is revoked after the operation. No POD validation exemption exists.
