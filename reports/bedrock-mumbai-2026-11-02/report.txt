Mumbai Bedrock availability and prices — 2 November 2026

Read-only audit. No models, configuration, permissions or deployments changed.
No inference requests made in this audit; catalogue/access checks are not a
legal-drafting benchmark.

Calls made:
  aws bedrock list-foundation-models --region ap-south-1
  aws bedrock list-inference-profiles --region ap-south-1
  aws pricing get-products --service-code AmazonBedrock
    --filters Type=TERM_MATCH,Field=regionCode,Value=ap-south-1 --region us-east-1
The last command accesses the pricing catalogue, not model inference. No client
information or prompts are sent to the US pricing endpoint.

Results: 87 model entries; 46 expose ON_DEMAND directly in Mumbai (42 text-output
models and four embedding models). The other entries are profile/provisioned
options. No assumption that a Mumbai price proves Mumbai availability: models
were selected from ListFoundationModels first, then joined to pricing SKUs.

Plausible shortlist (an engineering assessment, not measured legal quality):
- Short subject lines/options: Ministral 3 8B. Compact, supports structured
  outputs; USD 0.18 input / 0.18 output per million tokens.
- Stronger candidate for both tasks: Qwen3 235B A22B Instruct 2507. Instruction
  tuned, multilingual, 256K context; USD 0.26 / 1.04.
- Notice drafting: Mistral Large 3. Reasoning/multilingual model with 256K context
  and 32K max output; USD 0.59 / 1.76. Compare with Qwen on approved examples.
All three returned AUTHORIZED, entitlement AVAILABLE, agreement AVAILABLE and
region AVAILABLE from GetFoundationModelAvailability in this account. Quality,
latency, schema adherence and factual fidelity still need workload evaluation.
The advocate-review requirement remains essential to the existing flow.

Prices below are USD per 1,000,000 text tokens, standard on-demand, excluding tax,
cache, batch, flex, priority and provisioned pricing. AWS Price List unit 1K tokens
was multiplied by 1000. Claude/Cohere use the pricing page's Mumbai feed because
the matching rates were absent from AmazonBedrock GetProducts. Claude 3 entries
show the published base tariff; they are LEGACY, with catalogue EOL 13 Nov 2026
and an extended-access flag. Do not base a new deployment or a guaranteed legacy
billing quote on those entries. No special extended tariff was verified.

Inference profiles: 46 returned. APAC routes include regions outside India;
global profiles are not India-restricted. Six India-only profiles list exactly
Mumbai (ap-south-1) and Hyderabad (ap-south-2):
  in.openai.gpt-5.6-terra
  in.openai.gpt-5.6-luna
  in.anthropic.claude-sonnet-5
  in.anthropic.claude-opus-5
  in.anthropic.claude-haiku-4-5-20251001-v1:0
  in.moonshotai.kimi-k3
These do NOT meet the direct-Mumbai/no-profile requirement and are excluded from
the price table/shortlist. Their existence corrects the broader idea that every
cross-region profile must leave India. Current application adapter deliberately
rejects every profile; this audit does not change that behaviour.
Nova Lite and Claude 3.5 Sonnet still do not offer direct Mumbai invocation in
the returned catalogue. This does not mean Mumbai has no usable alternatives.

Evidence in this directory:
- foundation-models.json: complete returned foundation-model catalogue.
- inference-profile-regions.json: every profile and its destination regions.
  Empty region string in a global-profile ARN denotes global scope; it is not
  interpreted as India-only.
- direct-model-prices.csv: all 46 direct models with exact model IDs and prices.
- price-evidence.json: matched Mumbai SKUs/rates and their publication dates.
